# Giving Back
This page is dedicated to the outreach projects I have worked on. Outreach is an important aspect of my work, as it allows me to share knowledge, inspire others, and contribute to the community. Below are some of the key outreach initiatives I have been involved in.

## Outreach Projects
### [Career Girls - AI, Machine Learning, and Robotics Virtual Camp](https://www.careergirls.org/2023-ai-machine-learning-and-robotics-virtual-camp/)
In 2023, while I was interning at Toyota Research Institute, I had the privilege of participating in the Career Girls event, where I presented my experience in AI, machine learning, and robotics, and served as a role-model expert.
### [Girls in Control Workshop](https://www.ifac-control.org/areas/girls-in-control-gic-workshop-and-material) 
Organized under International Federation of Automatic Control (IFAC), Girls in Control (GIC) is a workshop and material series aimed at inspiring young women ro explore the field of control systems and robotics. We organized a workshop for Turkish middle school students in 2021, online.
