  # Hello. I am Ruya.

  I received my PhD in the Electrical and Computer Engineering Department at the University of Michigan - Ann Arbor, where I am advised by Professor [Necmiye Ozay](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~necmiye/). My research is mainly focused on **combination of formal methods and machine learning**, where I am trying to combine the advantages of machine learning with formal methods to infer better specifications for systems. I am also interested in **personalization**, **safety guarantees**, and **control synthesis** of cyber-physical systems, especially in the context of autonomous vehicles.
